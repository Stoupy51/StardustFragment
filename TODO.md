
Stardust Spawner machine :
- Takes a mob soul as input
- Spawns a mob with the soul's properties
- Animation: start scale 0 with NoAI, progress to scale 1 then enable AI
- 3 tiers of delay (button inside GUI) : (100 Kw for 60s per mob, 1000 kW for 30s per mob, 5000 kW for 15s per mob)
- Can't have two Stardust Spawner machines with the same mob soul in a distance of 128 blocks

Mob essence can be obtained by killing 10 mobs of a type with a special item in offhand:
- Right click on the target mob (any hand)
- Hold in offhand and kill 10 times the mob (it will update custom_data and lore to see the mob type and progress)
- Excluded mob types: Non-alive, Wither, Ender Dragon, Player, Armor Stand
